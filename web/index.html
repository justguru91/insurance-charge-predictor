The workflow you can explain in your viva

Load CSV→Clean & validate→Split 80/20→5-fold comparison→Test once→Save & predict

Numeric features are imputed and scaled within each training fold. Sex, smoker status, and region are one-hot encoded. The complete preprocessing pipeline is saved with the model.

Dataset scope and source

Medical Cost Personal / insurance.csv, distributed with the datasets for Brett Lantz's Machine Learning with R. These historical US records do not represent current Indian insurance prices. The source does not establish a current tariff or policy period.

View the public source ↗

Small benchmark data, limited recorded categories, possible sampling bias, no external validation. The app does not make policy eligibility or underwriting decisions.
